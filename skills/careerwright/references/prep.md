# Company prep notes

Load when writing `prep.md` for an interview.

```
# <Company>: <role> prep (<date>)

## The company
- What they sell, to whom, and how they make money. (source, date)
- Size, funding or public status, recent layoffs or hires. (source, date)
- Last six months of news. (source, date)

## The team
- Stack from the posting, engineering blog, GitHub org. (source)
- Who interviews (from the invite), and their public work if any.

## The process
- Rounds, take-home or live coding, reported length (Glassdoor, levels.fyi, Blind; say how many reports).

## My stories
- Map the posting's top needs to the person's STAR stories (one story can serve two questions).

## Questions to ask them
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...
```

Rules: every fact gets a link and a date; say "not found" rather than guess; salary bands from the posting or a public pay-transparency law beat aggregator numbers.

Structured behavioural interviews predict job performance better than unstructured ones (Schmidt and Hunter 1998; Levashina et al. 2014), which is why most companies use STAR-shaped questions.
